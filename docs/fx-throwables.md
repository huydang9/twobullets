# Throwable effects (frag, smoke, molotov, flashbang)

**Networked play (protocol v9) uses the same effects.** Online, the match server decides *when and where* — `EquipmentSystem`
runs in `serverThrowables` mode: the hands, cook timer, aim arc and throw sounds stay local, but the grenade, the
detonation, the smoke cloud, the fire area and the flash arrive as `ThrowableUpdate` ops (`apps/client/src/net/NetThrowables.ts`)
and are fed into the same observables this document describes. Clouds and fire areas are rebuilt from the server's seed,
so every client sees the same volume the server tested sight lines against. See `docs/backend/netcode.md` §8.4.

Realistic detonation, smoke and fire visuals built from CC0 flipbooks. They replace the procedural atlas sprites that
looked fake. Gameplay is untouched: the smoke volume and fire area come from the shared rules (`smokePuffs`,
`FirePatch.cells`), and the public API of `EquipmentPresentation` is the same.

Code: `apps/client/src/equipment/presentation/`

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `VfxBatch.ts` | Thin-instanced flipbook quads for one sheet: sprite, streak, decal and upright modes, frame blending, soft fades, fog, optional back-to-front sort, lit decals |
| `VfxParticles.ts` | CPU particle pool that draws through a `VfxBatch`, plus `VfxRecord` for renderers that push quads directly |
| `VfxLibrary.ts` | The 7 textures, 7 batches (one draw call each), 6 pools, caps, alpha order, preset quality |
| `vfxLighting.ts` | Sun and sky lighting converted to display space (exposure → ACES → gamma), shared by every lit effect |
| `vfxRandom.ts` | Seeded PRNG (mulberry32), position seeds, cone sampling |
| `vfxManifest.ts` | Generated by the pipeline: URL, hash, grid, cell UV size, fps, loop, blend, mean luma, atlas cells |
| `ExplosionEffects.ts` | Frag, flashbang, smoke pop, molotov shatter |
| `SmokeRenderer.ts` | Smoke grenade clouds (flipbook sprites outside, analytic volume inside) |
| `FireRenderer.ts` | Molotov fire patches |
| `fxPools.ts` | `EquipmentFx` now also owns `vfx` (the library) and `scorch` (lit decals) |

`FlashOverlay.ts` (the HUD-driven white-out and afterimage) is unchanged.

## Assets and pipeline

`node tools/vfx/build.mjs [--only=explosion,smokeCloud] [--preview]` reads `assets-src/` and writes
`apps/client/public/assets/vfx/*.ktx2`, `credits.json` and `vfxManifest.ts`. One worker encodes one texture at a time,
and the script aborts itself after 10 minutes. `--preview` decodes every output with the hosted Babylon transcoder
(the same one the browser uses), checks for mips and linear transfer, and writes PNG contact sheets to
`assets-src/vfx/preview/`.

| Output | Source (all CC0) | Size | Grid | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `explosion.ktx2` | Unity Labs Explosion01 | 1024² | 5×5, 204 px cells | 401 KB |
| `explosion-dust.ktx2` | Unity Labs Explosion01-nofire | 512² | 5×5, 102 px | 114 KB |
| `smoke-cloud.ktx2` | Unity Labs Cloud01 | 1024² | 8×8, 128 px | 564 KB |
| `smoke-wispy.ktx2` | Unity Labs WispySmoke01 | 512² | 8×8, 64 px | 167 KB |
| `flame.ktx2` | Unity Labs Flame03 | 1024×512 | 16×4, 64×128 px | 234 KB |
| `particles.ktx2` | Kenney trace_01, dirt_01/02 (blobs cut out), circle_05, flare_01, star_04, light_01 | 512² | 4×4 atlas | 98 KB |
| `scorch.ktx2` | Poly Haven burned_ground_01 (albedo + AO), masked by Kenney scorch_01 / scorch_03 | 1024×512 | 2 cells | 395 KB |

The total download is about 2 MB. On the GPU they take about 5 MB as BC7/ASTC (UASTC transcodes at 8 bpp, plus mips).

Conventions:

- **POT canvases with whole-pixel cells from the top-left.** The Explosion sheet is resampled to 5 × 204 = 1020 px, so
  every cell edge lands on a texel edge. It is then padded to 1024. Non-POT sizes such as 1020 break compressed mips
  (level 1 would be 510, which isn't a multiple of 4). The manifest gives `cellU`/`cellV`.
- **Premultiplied alpha, linear transfer.** The TGAs are straight alpha with garbage RGB under zero alpha. Sharp
  resamples premultiplied, then the pipeline premultiplies in byte space. The KTX2 has no sRGB flag, so the shader
  samples exactly the stored display-space values (the FX shaders write after tone mapping), and mips have no dark
  fringes. Blend mode is `ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`, and a per-quad `additive` scales the output alpha. Fire (additive)
  and smoke (alpha) can therefore share one draw call.
- **Frame 0 is the top-left cell. V = 0 is the top row.** Babylon doesn't flip compressed textures, and `invertY: false`
  keeps image fallbacks the same.
- **`meanLuma`** is the alpha-weighted mean luma of each sheet's straight colors. A lit tint is
  `targetDisplayColor / meanLuma`, so the flipbook's baked shading keeps its contrast but lands at the scene's brightness.

## What each throwable does

Counts are for the High preset. Balanced uses about 0.65× and Performance about 0.4× (`VfxLibrary.quality`, read from
`graphicsOf(scene)` on every spawn, so a preset change applies immediately). Every detonation reseeds the RNG from its
position and a counter.

### Frag: 76 particles, 1 decal, 4 draw calls (particles, explosion, dust, decals)

| Part | Details |
|---|---|
| Flash core + flare | Additive glow grows 0.6 → 3.4 m in 0.14 s; horizontal flare 0.09 s |
| Fireball | 3 Explosion01 sprites: a main one 1.3 m up the normal (2.1 → 3.0 m half size, 2.4 s) and two offset lobes. Frames ease out (`framePower` 1.9), so the fire burns for about 0.5 s and the smoke rolls on. Starts 35% additive, rotation ±0.3 rad, scale ×0.9–1.15 |
| Dust ring | 10 nofire sprites race out along the ground at 6–10 m/s with drag, growing to about 2.4 m, lit brown dust, 2.4–3.2 s. Grounded detonations only |
| Lingering dust | 4 slow rising sprites, 3.2–4 m, 5–7 s |
| Sparks | 30 `trace_01` streaks stretched along velocity (16–42 m/s, gravity) |
| Debris | 22 soil chunks (4 cut-out shapes, spinning) and 5 dirt-spray sprites |
| Scorch | Lit `burned_ground` blast mark, 3–3.8 m across, 45 s, fades out over the last 10 s |
| Light, shake | Unchanged (`EquipmentLights.flash`, `CameraShake`) |

### Smoke grenade: ≤ 200 cloud sprites + ≤ ~35 jet + 6 pop, 1–2 draw calls (smoke batch, inside pass)

- **Volume:** each of the 10 gameplay puffs (`smokePuffs`, so radius follows `smokeRadius(t)`, drift and wall probes
  included) gets a seeded cluster of 20 Cloud01 sprites. One wide core sprite plus 19 spread inside the sphere, biased
  to the rim. They orbit slowly and animate at 6 fps with per-sprite offsets. The layout is seeded from the cloud seed,
  so every client sees the same shapes.
- **Light:** sun side brighter, shade side from the sky, and the underside picks up ground bounce. Everything is
  converted to display space and divided by the sheet's mean luma.
- **Soft:** sprites fade against the cloud's ground plane (0.9 m) and when nearer the camera than their size. This is
  the depth fade without a scene depth prepass (see *Trade-offs*).
- **End:** density fades with the rules over the last 6 s. The sprites also drift apart (+30% spread), grow (+35%) and
  thin out.
- **Occlusion:** core sprites are near opaque (0.95 × a solid texture core), and a line of sight through a grown cloud
  crosses many layers. A player inside is hidden from outside.
- **Inside:** when the camera enters a puff, that puff's sprites fade (by up to 70%) and the existing analytic
  fullscreen pass integrates the same soft density, weighted in by depth. Walking in doesn't pop.
- **Debug:** `settings.smokeDebug` still draws the gameplay spheres (magenta) as analytic billboards instead of sprites.
- **Canister jet:** flipbook puffs spray from the grenade while the cloud grows (first 4 s).

### Molotov: 4 draw calls (flame, particles, wispy, decals)

| Part | Details |
|---|---|
| Shatter | 26 glass shards (cut-out shapes, pale green, 35% additive glint), 10 glints, 7 Flame03 whoomp sprites, a warm light |
| Flames | Per burning cell of the shared `FirePatch` (≤ 40 cells on the 1 m grid): 3 upright Flame03 billboards (1 on Performance), jittered over the cell so neighbours interleave. Half width 0.3 / 0.2–0.28 m (flames 0.8–1.2 m tall), own frame offset and speed, swaying tip, 82% additive. At most 600 per frame across all patches |
| Glow | Additive ground glow per cell |
| Embers | Rising streaks, rate capped per patch |
| Plume | Dark WispySmoke puffs from 1.3 m up, rising and growing to about 5 m, 4–6 s, drawn before the flames so bright tips stay on top |
| Burn mark | Lit burned-ground decal per cell (1.6–2 m across). Lives for the cell's burn time plus 28 s |
| Light | Unchanged flickering fire light (`requestFire`) |

The shared rules use a slope- and wall-aware cell flood, not PUBG's 9 discs (`docs/equipment/design.md` §12), so the
flames follow `FirePatch.cells`.

### Flashbang: 29 particles, 1 decal, 3 draw calls (particles, dust, decals)

A white burst (0.4 → 4.5 m in 0.16 s), a core, a flare and a star, 22 sparks, 3 light dust puffs and a faint mark, plus
the existing bright light. The white-out and afterimage stay in `FlashOverlay` (unchanged).

## Draw order, pools and cost

alphaIndex in rendering group 0 (`VFX_ALPHA_INDEX`):

- blood and atlas decals 0
- **vfx decals 0.5**
- atlas alpha 1
- atlas additive 2
- **particles 2.2 · wispy 2.3 · flame 2.4 · explosion 2.5 · dust 2.6 · smoke 3**
- analytic smoke 4 / 4.5
- throw arc 5

Smoke, dust, wispy and the explosion sort back to front within their batch (an in-place shell sort over packed
distance keys, with no allocation).

| Batch | Cap | Pool | Sorted | Features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| particles | 1600 | 1000 | no | atlas, fog |
| explosion | 48 | 48 | yes | frame blend, soft, fog |
| dust | 420 | 360 | yes | frame blend, soft, fog |
| wispy | 700 | 640 | yes | frame blend, loop, soft, fog |
| flame | 900 | 160 | no | frame blend, loop, fog |
| smoke | 2800 | 400 | yes | frame blend, loop, soft, fog |
| decals | 160 | ring | no | lit, fog, zOffset -2 |

- **No per-frame allocations.** Measured headless (NullEngine) at 0.1 B/frame steady state with a grown cloud, a
  burning patch and live pools. Per-sprite values go through records, not loose doubles. Float uniforms are only set
  when they change (ShaderMaterial boxes every `setFloat`). Smoke lighting recomputes only when the sun, sky fill or
  exposure change.
- **GPU estimate (not measured yet: needs `?bench=v1` in a real tab).**
  - A frag burst at 10 m is about 5 M fragments at 1080p: 3 fireball sprites up to about 6 m, a dust ring, and 2
    texture taps per fragment. That is roughly 0.5–1 ms on an M2 Pro, shrinking with distance.
  - A grown smoke cloud at 20 m covers about a third of the screen, about 8 sprites deep (about 5 M fragments, ~0.5 ms).
  - Standing inside is the analytic pass (about 1 ms at 1440p) plus faded sprites.
  - Clouds beyond 90 m draw half their sprites, 20% larger.

## Trade-offs and known limits

- **Soft particles without scene depth.** A depth prepass would redraw Map v1. Sprites instead fade against a floor
  height (the detonation or cloud ground) and near the camera. A sprite that crosses a wall or a slope that isn't the
  floor still shows a hard line; this is rare for smoke, because wall probes keep puffs off walls.
- **Decals are lit, but not shadowed.** A scorch in shade reads a little brighter than the shaded ground around it.
  They are flat quads on the hit normal (as in `docs/fx-blood.md`), so they can overhang a sharp crest.
- **Flipbook lighting is baked from the upper left.** Rotation is limited to ±0.3–0.7 rad on smoke and the fireball, so
  the light direction doesn't flip. Dust ring sprites rotate freely, since that dust is low-contrast.
- **No heat distortion.** It needs a scene color copy every frame.
- **Loading.** If the KTX2 decoder can't load (it is configured by `AssetLibrary` before the presentation is built), the
  batches draw nothing and the console warns `[vfx] … failed to load`.

## DEV console

```js
const p = __twobullets.presentation;
p.debugExplosion("frag", 8)   // detonation effect on the ground 8 m ahead (also "flash", "smoke", "molotov")
p.debugThrow("frag")          // full throw with hands; also "smoke", "flash", "molotov"
p.debugSmoke(9)               // cloud 9 m ahead (35 s)
p.debugMolotov(6)             // fire patch 6 m ahead (= p.equipment.debugFire(6))
p.debugFlash(1)               // white-out only
p.equipment.debugClear()
p.equipment.stats()           // vfx quads per batch, pool usage, smokeSprites, scorchDecals
p.equipment.settings.smokeDebug = true   // gameplay puffs as magenta spheres
```

`?quality=balanced` or `?quality=performance` shows the reduced counts.

To rebuild the textures after changing the pipeline, run `node tools/vfx/build.mjs --preview` and check the PNGs in
`assets-src/vfx/preview/`.
